Why water damage in Colorado is different

The Front Range is one of the most hail-prone areas in the country. Denver, Aurora and Colorado Springs see damaging hail most summers. Cracked shingles or skylights often leak days later. Afternoon storms can also turn dry creeks into torrents. The September 2013 floods hit Boulder, Lyons and Estes Park hard.

Winter adds frozen pipes, most often when it drops from the 50s to below zero in a day. Basements are common along the Front Range, so a burst pipe or a failed sump pump often means a finished basement full of water. Mountain homes and rentals sit empty for weeks, so a small leak can run for days. Wyoming and Utah share many of these risks.

Tips for Colorado homeowners

  • Shut off outside spigots and remove hoses before the first hard freeze.
  • Put water sensors and an auto shut-off valve in rental and vacation homes.
  • Have the roof checked after any hail storm.
  • Test your sump pump and add a battery backup if the basement is finished.

Insurance and licensing in Colorado

The Colorado Division of Insurance regulates home insurance in Colorado. It takes complaints if a claim stalls. Many Colorado policies use a percentage deductible for wind and hail. A roof claim may cost you more than a pipe burst.

Most home policies exclude flood. In Colorado, flood cover comes from FEMA's flood program (NFIP) or a private insurer. NFIP policies usually take 30 days to start.

Colorado has no statewide general contractor license. Cities and counties such as Denver license builders. Plumbers and electricians hold state licenses.

Water damage questions in Colorado

Do you cover all of Colorado?

Yes. DryBeacon takes calls from all of Colorado, including Denver, Colorado Springs, Aurora, Fort Collins and Lakewood. Small towns and rural areas in every county are covered too.

Does homeowners insurance cover flood damage in Colorado?

Usually not. Most home policies in Colorado exclude rising water. They often cover sudden leaks, like a burst pipe. Flood needs a separate NFIP or private policy. For claim help, contact the Colorado Division of Insurance.

Does a water damage company need a license in Colorado?

Colorado has no statewide general contractor license, so local rules apply. Plumbing and electrical work still need licensed trades, and your city may require permits for rebuilds.

Is a frozen pipe burst covered in Colorado?

Usually, if it was sudden and the home was kept heated. Policies may deny freeze claims if the house was left unheated or vacant for a long time. Keep heat on and shut off water before long trips.
