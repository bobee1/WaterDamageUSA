Editorial Policy

Our guides help people make decisions during a stressful event, so they have to be accurate, plain and honest about what they don't cover.

Document: DryBeacon Editorial Policy · Effective November 18, 2026 · Responsible editor: Anjam Javed

Who writes our content

Pages and guides are published under the byline DryBeacon Editorial. The editorial team researches each topic, drafts the page, checks it against its sources and reviews it before it goes live. Anjam Javed, the owner of DryBeacon, is responsible for what we publish.

We use software tools, including AI writing tools, to help research and draft content. Every page is checked by a person against the sources listed on it before it is published. Tools never decide what facts appear on a page.

Which sources we use

We prefer primary and official sources, in this order:

  1. Federal agencies: the U.S. Environmental Protection Agency (EPA), the Federal Emergency Management Agency (FEMA) and the National Flood Insurance Program, the Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC), and the National Weather Service.
  2. State and local government: state insurance departments, state statutes, licensing boards, and city or county building and utility departments.
  3. Industry standards: published standards such as ANSI/IICRC S500 for water damage restoration and ANSI/IICRC S520 for mold remediation, cited by name.
  4. Company sources: used only for a single figure, credited by name in the text.

We open and read every source before citing it. A search result snippet is never treated as a source.

How we check facts

  • Every number, deadline and legal rule on a page is traced to a source listed on that page.
  • Laws are cited with their section numbers and effective dates, and checked to make sure they are still in force on the publish date.
  • Health topics use public-health agency guidance only, and we tell readers to talk with a doctor about symptoms.
  • Insurance and legal topics carry a "general information, not advice" note.

What we don't do

  • We don't publish fake reviews, ratings, testimonials or case studies.
  • We don't invent statistics or prices. Cost information is limited to the factors that change a price.
  • We don't claim credentials, awards or affiliations we don't hold.
  • We don't let a payment from any company change what a guide says.
  • We don't present stock photos as photos of our own jobs.

Review schedule

Each page shows when it was last reviewed. We review pages at least once every 12 months, and sooner when a law, building code or major guidance changes, or after a large flood or storm season shows something we should add.

How we're paid and how that affects content

DryBeacon earns money from restoration jobs that start with a call or form request. That is why every page has a phone number. It does not change the facts in our guides. When a reader's situation doesn't need a restoration crew, such as a small spill that can be dried with fans, our guides say so.

Corrections

If you see an error, outdated rule or broken link, please tell us through the contact page or by calling 1-800-555-0199. We review every report. Material corrections are made promptly and the page's review date is updated.
