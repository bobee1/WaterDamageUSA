Ceiling Water Damage Repair

A water stain on the ceiling means water is getting in from above. DryBeacon crews find the source, dry the ceiling and attic, and repair the damage.

Quick answer: Ceiling water damage repair starts by finding and fixing the leak above, then drying the ceiling, insulation and framing before replacing stained or sagging drywall and repainting. Painting over a wet stain only hides the problem. Because wet ceiling drywall can fail, a sagging or dripping ceiling should be handled the same day.

What ceiling water damage repair includes

  • Finding the source with moisture meters and thermal imaging
  • Safe draining of water trapped above sagging drywall
  • Removing wet insulation from the attic or floor cavity above
  • Drying ceiling joists, subfloor and attic sheathing
  • Cutting out and replacing damaged ceiling drywall
  • Texture matching (smooth, knockdown or popcorn) and stain-blocking primer
  • Paint to blend the repair
  • Mold check of the area above the ceiling

The ceiling repair process

  1. Make it safe. Power to ceiling fixtures in the area is turned off, and the room below is protected.
  2. Find and stop the leak. A plumber or roofer fixes the source; the restoration crew confirms it with readings.
  3. Relieve trapped water. Water held above a bulge is drained in a controlled way.
  4. Open and dry. Wet drywall and insulation are removed, and the cavity above is dried with air movers and dehumidifiers.
  5. Replace and finish. New drywall goes in, texture is matched, and stain-blocking primer and paint finish the job.

Can a water-damaged ceiling be saved?

Sometimes. A small stain from a leak that was fixed quickly, on drywall that stayed firm and dried fully, may only need stain-blocking primer and paint. The drywall should be replaced when it:

  • sags, bulges or feels soft
  • has seams or tape pulling away
  • crumbles when pressed
  • shows mold, or the area above has mold
  • was soaked by sewage, such as a toilet overflow from the floor above

Older plaster ceilings and popcorn texture need extra care: popcorn texture in homes built before the 1980s may contain asbestos and should be tested before it is disturbed.

Ceiling leaks in condos and apartments

When water comes through your ceiling from the unit above, tell your building manager or association and your neighbor right away. Responsibility often depends on where the leak started and on the building's rules and master insurance policy. Document everything with photos and keep notes on who you contacted and when.

How to prevent ceiling water damage

  • Have the roof inspected after major storms and every few years.
  • Keep gutters and downspouts clear so water drains away from the roof edge.
  • Replace cracked caulk and grout around tubs and showers.
  • Have attic AC drain lines cleared and add a float switch to the drain pan.
  • Insulate attic pipes and follow cold-weather tips from the American Red Cross to prevent frozen pipes.

What affects the cost of ceiling water damage repair

  • Size of the damaged area and whether the ceiling collapsed.
  • The source repair, such as roofing or plumbing, billed separately.
  • Insulation and attic drying above the ceiling.
  • Texture matching and ceiling height.
  • Mold or asbestos testing, if needed.

DIY or professional?

A small, dry, old stain on firm drywall is a reasonable DIY primer-and-paint job once the cause is fixed. Call a professional for an active leak, a sagging or soft ceiling, a stain that keeps growing, or any ceiling leak you cannot trace. Wet ceilings often hide wet insulation and framing that need drying first.

Ceiling water damage questions

Is a water stain on the ceiling an emergency?

A dry, old stain is not an emergency, but it means water got in at some point and may again. A stain that is growing, wet to the touch, dripping or sagging needs attention the same day, because ceiling drywall can fail and the source is still active.

Can I just paint over a ceiling water stain?

Only after the leak is fixed and the ceiling and the space above it are confirmed dry. Painting over a wet stain hides an active problem and can trap moisture. Use a stain-blocking primer before the finish coat.

What should I do if my ceiling is sagging with water?

Keep people and pets out of the room, move furniture and electronics away, and put a bucket and plastic down. Turn off power to ceiling lights in that area from the breaker panel. Do not stand directly under the bulge. Call for help to drain and repair it safely.

How do I know where a ceiling leak is coming from?

Think about what is above the stain: a bathroom, a roof valley, an attic AC unit or a water line. Stains that appear after rain point to the roof; stains that grow after showers or flushing point to plumbing. Moisture meters and thermal cameras confirm the source.

Does insurance cover ceiling water damage?

It often does when the cause is sudden, like a burst pipe or storm damage to the roof. Damage from a long-term slow leak or an aging roof may be excluded. Check your policy and photograph the stain and its source.
