Document: DryBeacon Privacy Policy · Effective {{effective}} · Owner and operator: {{owner}}, doing business as {{brand}} ({{domain}})

1. Who this policy covers

This policy applies to {{domain}}, our toll-free phone line ({{phone}}) and any text messages or emails about a water damage request. In this policy, "{{brand}}", "we" and "us" mean {{owner}}, doing business as {{brand}}. "You" means anyone who visits the site, calls, texts or sends a form.

2. Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Contact form: your name, phone number, ZIP code, any message you type, the page you sent it from, and the date and time you agreed to be contacted.
  • Phone calls: your phone number, the number you called, the time and length of the call, the ZIP code or location you share, and details about the damage. Calls may be recorded (see section 5).
  • Text messages: the content of texts you send us and your replies to ours.

Information collected automatically

  • Server and security logs: our host, Cloudflare, processes your IP address, browser type and the pages requested so the site can load and stay protected from abuse.
  • Analytics: if we turn on site analytics, we use a privacy-focused tool that counts visits without advertising cookies and without building a profile of you across other websites.

We do not knowingly collect Social Security numbers, payment card numbers, health records or precise GPS location through this website.

3. How we use information

  • To respond to your request and arrange water damage restoration at your property.
  • To call or text you about your request, including follow-up about scheduling or the job.
  • To check the quality of calls, train staff and resolve disputes.
  • To keep the website secure, prevent fraud and fix technical problems.
  • To meet legal duties, such as record-keeping and responding to lawful requests.

4. Who we share information with

  • Restoration providers: we share your name, phone number, ZIP code and job details with the restoration crew or contractor that serves your area so they can contact you and do the work. Some providers pay {{brand}} for these jobs.
  • Service providers: companies that help us run the business, such as website hosting (Cloudflare), email delivery (Resend) and phone and call-tracking services. They may use your information only to provide services to us.
  • Legal and safety reasons: when the law requires it, to protect rights and safety, or to investigate fraud.
  • Business transfers: if {{brand}} is sold or merged, information may move to the new owner under this policy.

Under some state laws, sharing contact details with a provider in exchange for payment can count as a "sale" of personal information. You can opt out at any time (see section 8).

5. Call recording

Calls to {{phone}} may be recorded for quality, training and record-keeping. Federal law (18 U.S.C. § 2511(2)(d)) allows recording when one party consents, but several states require every party to agree, including California (Penal Code § 632), Florida (Fla. Stat. § 934.03), Illinois (720 ILCS 5/14-2), Maryland (Cts. & Jud. Proc. § 10-402), Massachusetts (G.L. c. 272, § 99), Montana, New Hampshire, Pennsylvania (18 Pa.C.S. § 5704) and Washington (RCW 9.73.030). For that reason, every caller hears a recording notice at the start of the call. If you do not want the call recorded, tell us and we will not record it, or use the contact form instead.

6. Calls and texts

When you tick the consent box on our form, you agree that {{brand}} and a restoration provider serving your area may call or text you about your request, including by automated means. This consent follows the Telephone Consumer Protection Act (47 U.S.C. § 227). It is not a condition of any purchase. Reply STOP to any text to stop texts, or ask us on a call to stop calling. We do not sell your number to telemarketers.

7. How long we keep information

We keep request records and call recordings only as long as needed for the purposes in section 3, to handle warranty or billing questions, and to meet legal duties. Then we delete them or remove the details that identify you.

8. Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • know what personal information we hold about you and how we use it;
  • get a copy of it;
  • correct it;
  • delete it;
  • opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of it, and of targeted advertising.

Residents of California have these rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act, as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act (Cal. Civ. Code § 1798.100 and following). Residents of other states with consumer privacy laws, such as Virginia, Colorado, Connecticut, Texas and Oregon, have similar rights. We extend the same basic choices to every U.S. visitor, whether or not a state law requires it.

How to make a request: call {{phone}} or use the contact form and say "privacy request". We will confirm your identity by matching the phone number or details you gave us, then answer within 45 days. You may use an authorized agent. We honor Global Privacy Control browser signals as an opt-out request. We will not treat you differently for using your rights. If we deny your request, you can appeal by replying to our answer, and we will respond within the time your state's law sets.

9. Security

We use reasonable safeguards, including encrypted HTTPS connections, access limits and trusted service providers. No system is perfectly secure. If a data breach affects your personal information, we will notify you as required by the breach-notification law of your state. All 50 states have such a law.

10. Children

This website is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information, in line with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act. If you believe a child has sent us information, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Links to other websites

Our guides link to government agencies and other sources. Their privacy practices are their own, so please read their policies.

12. Changes to this policy

We will post any change on this page with a new effective date. Significant changes will be noted at the top of the page.
