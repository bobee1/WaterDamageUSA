General information, not professional advice

The guides, checklists and answers on {{domain}} explain water damage, drying, mold and insurance in general terms. They are not legal, insurance, medical, engineering or financial advice, and reading them does not create a professional relationship. Rules differ by state, county and city, and every insurance policy is written differently.

  • For coverage questions, read your policy and talk with your insurance agent or company.
  • For legal questions, such as a disputed claim or a landlord-tenant issue, talk with a lawyer licensed in your state.
  • For health concerns about mold or sewage exposure, talk with a doctor.
  • For structural concerns, such as a sagging ceiling or a shifted foundation, talk with a licensed contractor or structural engineer.

Response times

Our phone line is open 24/7. How soon a crew can reach a property depends on its location, the weather, road access, safety conditions and how many emergencies are happening at the same time, for example after a regional storm. We do not promise a specific arrival time.

Prices and estimates

We do not publish prices on this website. Any cost factors we describe are general. The price of a job is set in a written estimate or agreement after the property is inspected.

Licensing and permits

Licensing rules for restoration, mold, plumbing and construction work vary by state and city. Some states license mold assessors and mold remediators separately, and some require permits for drywall, electrical or plumbing repairs. Before any repair work, ask to see the licenses and permits your state and city require.
