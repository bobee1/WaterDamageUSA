Call for water damage help

Fastest option: call {{phone}}. Have your ZIP code ready, say where the water is coming from (pipe, appliance, roof, sewer or outside flooding), and tell us whether the source has been stopped.

Before you call: 4 quick safety steps

  1. Stay out of standing water if power is on. Water and live wiring are a deadly mix. Shut off power to wet rooms at the breaker panel only if you can reach it while standing on a dry floor.
  2. Stop the source. Close the main water shut-off valve for plumbing leaks, or the supply valve behind the toilet, sink or washer.
  3. Avoid sewage contact. Water from a toilet overflow or sewer backup can carry germs. Keep children and pets away and wear gloves and boots.
  4. Take photos. Photograph the water, damaged items and the source before cleanup. Your insurance company will likely ask for them.
