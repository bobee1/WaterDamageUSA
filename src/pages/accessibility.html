Our goal

{{brand}} aims to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at Level AA, published by the World Wide Web Consortium (W3C). These guidelines are widely used to judge whether websites are usable by people with disabilities.

What we've built in

  • A "Skip to content" link at the top of every page.
  • One clear heading structure on every page, so screen reader users can jump between sections.
  • Text and button colors chosen to meet a contrast ratio of at least 4.5 to 1.
  • A visible focus outline for keyboard users on every link, button and form field.
  • Text descriptions (alt text) for every meaningful image.
  • Form fields with visible labels and error messages announced to screen readers.
  • Pages that reflow on phones and tablets without sideways scrolling, and that respect a reduced-motion setting.
  • Phone numbers that work as tap-to-call links.

Known limits

Some outside websites we link to, such as government agencies, are not controlled by us and may not meet the same standard. If you find a page on our own site that doesn't work for you, we want to know.

Get help or report a barrier

If any part of this site is hard to use, call {{phone}} and we will give you the information by phone. You can also report the problem through the contact form. Please tell us the page address and what happened. We aim to reply within 5 business days and to fix confirmed problems as soon as we can.

Ongoing review

We test pages with keyboard navigation and automated checks when they are built, and review this statement at least once a year. Our editorial policy explains how we review the rest of our content.
