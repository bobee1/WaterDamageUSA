What DryFlare does

{{brand}} handles water damage restoration. That covers the work most people need after a leak, a burst pipe, a sewage backup or a flood: removing standing water, drying the structure, controlling mold growth and getting the property ready for repairs. We have been in business for {{years}} years.

Water damage is a race against time. Wet drywall, flooring and insulation start to break down within hours, and mold can begin to grow in 24 to 48 hours, according to the U.S. Environmental Protection Agency. That is why the first call matters so much, and why our line is open {{availability}}.

How a call to DryFlare works

  1. You call {{phone}}. Tell us what happened, where the water came from and your ZIP code.
  2. We size up the job. Clean water from a supply line is handled differently from sewage or floodwater, so we ask a few quick questions first.
  3. A local restoration crew is lined up. The job goes to a trained crew that works in your area, so help comes from nearby rather than from across the country.
  4. Drying and repairs begin. The crew inspects, removes water, sets up drying equipment and checks moisture until the structure is dry.

Who we help

  • Homeowners dealing with burst pipes, appliance leaks, roof leaks, basement flooding or sewage backups.
  • Renters and landlords who need water removed and drying started while the insurance and repair questions get sorted out.
  • Businesses and property managers who need water damage contained to keep a building open or reopen it sooner.
