Choosing among water damage restoration companies

When you compare water damage restoration companies, look for the things that protect you and your claim:

  • Fast response and a clear plan for stopping and removing the water.
  • Moisture readings at the start and end, so you and your insurer can see the building is dry.
  • A written estimate before work beyond emergency water removal.
  • Proper licenses where your state and city require them, especially for mold, plumbing, electrical and construction work.
  • Honest advice about what can be saved and what must be replaced.
